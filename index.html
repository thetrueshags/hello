Philosophy

Ideas should
touch everything.

Not stay in a silo.

I don't think in departments. A good idea about the product should inform the architecture. A constraint in engineering should reshape the design. The best outcomes happen when someone can hold the whole picture at once and actually act on all of it.

That's what drives me. Not efficiency for its own sake, but the satisfaction of watching an idea ripple across an entire business and turn into something real. I've been a founder, an engineer, a designer, a tester. Not because I was filling seats, but because I wanted to be in the room where each of those decisions was made.

When ideas can flow freely across every discipline instead of getting lost in handoffs, the impact compounds. That's how I work.
